Little Brush Support

Need help? Email support@littlebrush.app and we'll get back to you.

Where are the settings?

Tap the gear in the top corner and answer the grown-up question. Settings, child profiles, progress and storage are all there.

How do I add another child?

In the grown-ups area, tap Add a child. Each child gets their own gallery, tracing stars and settings. Tap a child's card to switch.

How do I save or print a picture?

Open My Gallery, tap a picture, then tap For grown-ups. You can save an image, make a print-ready PDF, rename or delete it. To save all of one child's pictures at once, open that child's Details in the grown-ups area and tap Save all pictures.

Will pictures be lost when I update the app?

No. Updates keep pictures. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so save copies first if you plan to uninstall.

Does Little Brush need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, including turning photos into coloring pages.

Privacy

Little Brush collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
