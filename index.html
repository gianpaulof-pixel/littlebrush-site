Made by a Parent, for Little Artists

Little Brush started with a simple idea: create a fun, distraction-free painting app for my two young daughters.

I wanted them to have a place to paint, color, and explore their creativity without advertisements, unnecessary pop-ups, or complicated menus.

What began as a little project for our family grew into Little Brush, an app designed to give young children the freedom to create at their own pace.
