Little Brush Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Little Brush is a painting, coloring and tracing app for young children. It was built so that children can create without ads, accounts or tracking. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

The short version

  • Little Brush does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information.
  • Little Brush has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • Everything a child makes stays on the device unless a parent chooses to save a copy.

What stays on the device

The app stores the following only on the device it is installed on, in the app's private storage:

  • Pictures children paint, and their names if a parent renames them.
  • Children's first names (or nicknames) and picture choices, if a parent adds child profiles.
  • Tracing progress for letters, numbers and shapes, a short list of recent activities shown in the parent area, and app settings.
  • Coloring pages a parent makes from a photo. Only the black-and-white outline is kept. The photo itself is processed on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

None of this information leaves the device. We (the developer) never receive it and cannot see it.

Photos

A parent can turn a photo into a coloring page. This happens entirely on the device using built-in software (including on-device face and subject detection). Photos are never uploaded. Opening a photo is behind the grown-up check and uses the device's own photo picker; the app does not ask for access to the whole photo library.

Internet access

Little Brush works fully offline. The app makes no network requests: its content, voice recordings and coloring pages are all included in the app. App stores may collect information about downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies.

Sharing and saving

Children cannot share anything from the app. A parent, after answering the grown-up question, can save or print a picture or save all of a child's pictures. That uses the device's own save or share menu, and the parent decides where the file goes.

Purchases, ads and links

Little Brush is a one-time purchase. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or links to websites inside the app.

Deleting information

Parents can delete individual pictures, all pictures, a child profile with its pictures and progress, or tracing progress from the grown-ups area. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Children's privacy

Little Brush is designed for children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to access, disclose or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that would collect information would require a new app version and would be explained here first.
